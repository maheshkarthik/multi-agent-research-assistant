# Sample Documents

14 PDFs for trying local research, copied from [Project 01](https://github.com/maheshkarthik/local-rag-document-assistant/tree/main/data/samples), where their contents and Markdown sources are described. They are released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Most are **fictional** (the Quillmark Analytics company documents, the product manual, the API reference, the lease, the travel guide and the research paper). The machine learning notes and the history of the printing press are factual. Keep this in mind in **Both** mode: research that mixes web sources with the fictional samples can combine real and invented facts.

Index them with:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m src.index
```
